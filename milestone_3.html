1. Optimization Algorithm & Underperformance Detection

Milestone 3 integrates the full analytical pipeline connecting raw ingested metrics to automated status classification. The engine evaluates campaigns continuously while enforcing the 3-day attribution lag shield.

1.1 Multi-Stage Classification Logic

To prevent erratic status flapping, campaigns transition across clearly defined operational states based on observation maturity and statistical evidence:

Campaign Status Trigger Criteria System Action & UI Behavior
Collecting Data $\text{Mature Days} < 7$ OR $\text{Conversions} < 5$ Neutral state. No underperformance or pause recommendations can be generated. Trajectory chart shows neutral blue trace.
Healthy $\text{Mature CPA} \le \text{Target CPA} \times 1.10$ with $\ge 80\%$ confidence Campaign performs within target efficiency. Eligible to receive 25% budget shifts from underperforming peers.
Watch $\text{Target CPA} \times 1.10 < \text{Mature CPA} \le \text{Target CPA} \times 1.25$ Early warning status. Heightened monitoring flag; alerts growth managers without triggering reallocation protocols.
Underperforming $\text{Mature CPA} > \text{Target CPA} \times 1.25$ AND $P(\text{CPA} > \text{Benchmark}) \ge 0.80$ Realized waste begins accumulating daily. Activates Funnel Diagnostic Engine and enqueues a 25% Fund Shift Recommendation.
Saturated Marginal CPA increases by > 35% following recent budget expansion Locks campaign from receiving further capital reallocations to avoid diminishing returns.

2. Fund-Shifting Recommendation Engine

Rather than prescribing abrupt campaign shutdowns, the recommendation engine follows the 25% Conservative Shift Protocol.

======================================================================================================== 25% FUND-SHIFTING DECISION ARCHITECTURE ======================================================================================================== [ Underperforming Campaign Identified ] | v [ Compute Daily Reallocation Capital ] -> Shift Amount = 0.25 * Current Daily Budget | +-------------------------------------------------+ | | v v [ High-Performing Peer Available? ] [ No Healthy Peer / Market Saturated ] | (YES) | (NO) v v [ Shift 25% to Winning Campaign ] [ Reallocate 25% to Offline Reserve Cash ] - Target: Lowest Mature CPA Campaign - Protects capital from paid ad inefficiency - Validates recipient headroom & capacity - Suggests redeployment to organic/retention | | +-------------------------------------------------+ | v [ Human Governance Decision Gate ] - Approve Shift (One-click human sign-off) - Snooze 7 Days (Allows further maturation) - Dismiss (Acknowledges risk with note) | v [ Mandatory 7-Day Observation Hold ] - Freezes further shifts on both campaigns - Prevents compounding budget volatility

2.1 Human-in-the-Loop Governance

  • Read-Only Safety: Recommendations never auto-execute. They generate clear, human-verifiable cards showing exact daily dollar amounts and mathematical justification.
  • Audit Logging: Every decision is permanently recorded with user identity, decision status, and before-and-after budget snapshots.

4. UI/UX Refinements Based on User Feedback

Following initial reviews, comprehensive design enhancements were implemented to remove cognitive clutter and ensure professional, real-world utility:

  1. Authentic Calendar Date Rollovers: Replaced synthetic date counters with real calendar calculations anchored to November 1, 2026. The simulation accurately rolls from November 30 to December 1, January 1, etc.
  2. Strict Day 0 Zero-Baseline State: All KPI gauges (Today's Spend, Total Mature Spend, Blended Mature CPA, Estimated Waste) and charts initialize cleanly at ₹0 on Day 0, accumulating organically as simulation days progress.
  3. Header De-Cluttering & Brand Refinement: Removed noisy decorative buttons (Maturity Shield, Product Review, 25% Protocol pills) and eliminated the pulsing green dot from the top logo. Standardized brand title to clean Ads Tracker.
  4. Introduction Landing Experience: Added a high-end editorial landing view detailing core value pillars, security guarantees, and a seamless "Get Started / Live Demo" onboarding path.
