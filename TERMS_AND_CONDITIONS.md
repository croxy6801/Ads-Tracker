Ad Waste Finder is a web-based data visualization and statistical analytics platform. The Service ingests campaign performance telemetry (via simulator engines, CSV data files, or read-only API connectors), applies mathematical Bayesian Beta-distribution models and deterministic rule filters, estimates theoretical ad spend waste, and presents suggestions for conservative budget reallocation (25% increments) and funnel diagnostic improvements.
