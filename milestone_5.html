2. Final Project Documentation & User Manual

2.1 Growth Operator User Manual

  1. Connecting Ad Accounts: Navigate to Settings > Add Provider. Authenticate with Google Ads or Meta via OAuth 2.0 with read-only permissions.
  2. Interpreting the Performance Trajectory Chart:
    • Solid Shaded Area (Mature History): Verified performance beyond the 72-hour lag window. CPA and waste figures are mathematically locked.
    • Striped Area (3-Day Holdout Window): Unsettled attribution lag window. Numbers will update as late conversion events arrive.
  3. Executing Budget Reallocations: When a campaign enters Underperforming, an actionable 25% Shift Card appears. Click Approve Shift to enact the change in your internal budget planner, or Diagnose to inspect funnel health.

3.2 Anticipated Viva Voce Questions & Answers

Q1: Why use a Bayesian approach over traditional p-value hypothesis testing?
Defense: Traditional p-values measure the probability of observing extreme data given a null hypothesis ($P(\text{Data} | H_0)$), but marketers need $P(\text{Campaign Underperforming} | \text{Observed Data})$. Bayesian posteriors directly answer this question and gracefully incorporate historical benchmark priors, preventing false alarms on low-conversion counts.
Q2: Why strictly limit budget reallocation to 25% increments?
Defense: Ad platform bidding algorithms (Smart Bidding / Meta Advantage+) rely on machine learning models that re-enter unstable "learning phases" if budgets change by > 20–30% overnight. The 25% shift maximizes capital protection while ensuring recipient campaigns remain algorithmically stable.
Q3: Why make the platform strictly read-only instead of automated auto-pause?
Defense: Fully automated write-back bots introduce substantial enterprise liability (runaway API bugs slashing budgets during seasonal sales). By enforcing a human-in-the-loop governance model, our platform provides evidence-based guidance with absolute zero execution risk.
