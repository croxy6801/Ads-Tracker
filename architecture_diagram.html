Architectural Guarantees

1. 72-Hour Holdout Shield
Prevents false-alarm cuts. Recent days are barred from triggering underperformance warnings until conversion maturity.
2. 25% Conservative Protocol
Reallocates spend in 25% tranches followed by 7-day holds, protecting machine-learning bidding algorithms from resets.
3. Bayesian Confidence ≥ 80%
Eliminates noise. Requires mathematical certainty and minimum 7 mature days before flagging campaigns.
4. Zero Write-Back Security
Read-only tokens exclusively. Zero automated account execution risk; full human agency over all budget reallocations.
